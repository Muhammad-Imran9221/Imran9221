## 👋 About Me

I’m **Muhammad Imran**, a **Biological Data Analyst** and **MS Botany** researcher. My work sits at the intersection of **plant science, quantitative genetics, and data science**. I analyze biological datasets using **Python**, **R**, and **machine learning**, with a strong focus on **GWAS, stress physiology, and phenotyping** in **bread wheat**.

- 🌱 Research: **Stress physiology**, **GWAS**, **morphology**, **trait association**
- 🧬 Currently: Developing **F1/F2 mapping populations** for bread wheat & hybrid characterization
- 📊 I love building: **analysis pipelines**, **visualizations**, and **reproducible research**
